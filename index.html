Nonno's Sarde A Beccafico

By: Giorgio Iacono

11/3/2025

    Ingredients

  • 10-20 Fresh Sardines
  • 1 Cup of Bread Crumbs
  • 1/4 cups of currants
  • 1/4 cups of Pinoli (Pine nuts)
  • 3 Cloves of Garlic
  • 1 tablespon of freshly chopped parsley
  • 3 to 4 anchovies, in fillets
  • Olive oil, 1/4 cup plus extra for baking the dish and drizzling
  • Salt and Pepper for taste
  • One lemon (use the juice)

    Instructions

  1. Approximately 30 minutes before preparing stuffing place currants in warm water to soak.
  2. Pre-heat oven to 400 degrees.
  3. In a large bowl combine bread crumbs, pinoli, garlic, parsley, anchovies and salt & pepper.
  4. 1/4 cups of Pinoli (Pine nuts)
  5. 3 Cloves of Garlic
  6. 1 tablespon of freshly chopped parsley
  7. 3 to 4 anchovies, in fillets
  8. Olive oil, 1/4 cup plus extra for baking the dish and drizzling
  9. Salt and Pepper for taste
  10. One lemon (use the juice)

This traditional Sicilian recipe, Sarde a Beccafico, is one of my Nonno’s favorites. It’s made with fresh sardines stuffed with breadcrumbs, pine nuts, and currants for a perfect mix of sweet and savory.

This dish is best enjoyed warm, served with a drizzle of olive oil and a squeeze of lemon. It’s a classic from Palermo that represents the heart of Sicilian home cooking.
